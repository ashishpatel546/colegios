# ⭐ Signature Features

> **Every feature below is real and working in the product today, not a roadmap promise.**

---

### 🔒 1. QR-Based Child Safety & Pickup

> **"No more uncertainty about who picked up which child."**

| Step | What happens |
|:---:|---|
| 1️⃣ | Parent generates a secure, one-time QR code from the mobile app |
| 2️⃣ | QR is shared with the authorised guardian collecting the child |
| 3️⃣ | Gate staff scan and verify before handing over the child |
| 4️⃣ | Parent gets an instant confirmation notification |
| 5️⃣ | Every pickup is logged with date and time for a full audit trail |

🛡️ **Dual verification with PIN authentication:** only authorised persons can pick up a child.

---

### 🪪 2. Secure QR-Based ID Cards

> **"Not a laminated photo anyone can forge."**

- 🖨️ **Batch printing:** a whole class or department in one PDF, branded with your school's logo
- ⚡ **Instant gate verification:** one scan shows the card is *valid, expired, replaced, revoked,* or the holder has left
- 🚫 **Lost-card protection:** reissuing a card instantly deactivates the old one
- 📜 **Full audit trail:** every card ever issued, who issued it, and why

---

### 🚪 3. QR-Based Visitor Management &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🎁 *FREE under current offer*

Every visitor (parent, vendor or courier) is logged with **name, mobile number and purpose of visit**. Records can be searched by name, mobile or date, with one-click CSV export for a security audit.

### 🚌 9. Transport Management & Live Bus Tracking &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🧪 *Beta* &nbsp;·&nbsp; 👑 *Free on Platinum for now*

> **"Every child, every bus, every stop, accounted for."**

| For | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🏫 **School** | Vehicles, routes and stops in one place, with driver and assistant history and reminders before vehicle documents expire |
| 💳 **Fees** | Transport fee set per stop and added to the student's fees automatically, on your school's own fee cycle. Exemptions go through approval and show up in fee reports |
| 🧑‍✈️ **Bus crew** | A simple Colegios Crew app for the driver and assistant: mark who boarded, who was dropped and who was handed to a guardian. The phone shares the bus location, with no GPS hardware to buy |
| 👨‍👩‍👧 **Parents** | Notified when their child boards, reaches school, is dropped or misses the bus. A "bus is almost at your stop" alert, and a live map during the trip |
| 🗺️ **Management** | A live map of every bus, today's trips at a glance, a full trip log, and an alert when a bus stops reporting |

🔒 **Privacy built in:** parents see the bus only during an active trip, and only until their own child is dropped.

🔗 **Linked to QR pickup:** in the evening, the crew hands a child to a parent or guardian at the stop. If someone else collects the child, the same QR-verified pickup is used.
